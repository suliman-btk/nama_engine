## Production log v6

Fixes the five issues raised on v5. Output: `production/lesson-01-render-out/lesson-01-v6.mp4`, ~16:25 (still inside the 5–20 minute policy).

- **Lip sync actually works now — this was a real bug, not a subtlety.** Two defects were found and fixed in `build_avatar_lottie.py`:
  1. *Keyframes eased instead of holding.* One keyframe was emitted per mouth-state change, so Lottie smoothly interpolated the mouth across the entire gap (often 300ms+). The mouth drifted between shapes rather than hitting them, which reads as no sync at all even though the timing was correct. Now each change emits a **pair** — the outgoing shape held until 2 frames before the cue, then a snap to the incoming shape — so the mouth lands on a shape and holds it, like real 2D animation.
  2. *An invalid keyframe silently deleted the whole mouth.* The first keyframe was written without easing fields (`{t, s}` only). That's legal only for the final keyframe; mid-sequence it makes lottie-web drop the entire mouth group — the character rendered with **no mouth at all**. Caught by rendering the generated Lottie in isolation and stepping through frames. All keyframes now carry easing.
  Verified frame-by-frame after the fix: the mouth visibly moves through thin line → wide open → round "O" → closed, in time with the audio.
  Also tried and reverted: stretching the mouth vertically to exaggerate openness. Combining a scale animation with the path-morph on the same layer makes lottie-web render blank frames, so openness is carried purely by the artist's own drawn shapes.
- **Sweater recoloured cream** (`#E9E1CE`) instead of ACT navy — a navy jumper on a navy slide made the character disappear into the background. Added a drop shadow rather than a light halo, so the light knit also has a defined edge on the cream/sand slides.
- **Presenter enlarged** from 360px to 560px; every card's right-hand gutter was widened to match so nothing collides.
- **Delivery no longer runs together.** Narration is now synthesised sentence by sentence with real silence spliced between (≈0.42s, ≈0.70s after a question, plus a short lead-in), instead of one continuous machine-paced take. This is why the runtime grew from 15:05 to ~16:25.
- **Arabic welcome is no longer translated.** Scene 3 was "{{arabic:bismillah}} — in the name of Allah. {{arabic:alhamdulillah}} — all praise is due to Allah…"; it now simply speaks the Arabic phrases, as an Arabic speaker would, with no English gloss appended. Updated in both `scenes.json` and `narration.md`.
